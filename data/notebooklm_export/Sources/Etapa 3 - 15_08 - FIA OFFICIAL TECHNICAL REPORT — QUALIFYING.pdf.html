FIA TECHNICAL DELEGATE REPORT

STAGE 3: FORMULA 1 GRAND PRIX OF AUSTRALIA 2026 — QUALIFYING SESSION From: The FIA Formula One Technical Delegate Date: August 15, 2026 To: All Teams, Paddock Press Document Ref: FIA-2026-ET3-Q01 Session: Qualifying (Sessão Única) Status: OFFICIAL CLASSIFICATION 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY The qualifying session for the Stage 3 Grand Prix of Australia, held at the Albert Park Circuit, Melbourne, has concluded. Following the completion of the session, the Technical Delegate hereby submits the official classifications and technical scrutineering notes. This session produced an unprecedented, historic milestone in modern Formula 1. The top three positions on the starting grid have been secured by a complete lockout of rookie Brazilian drivers:

  • frdsilva (Felipe) of KICK Sauber secured Pole Position with a lap time of 1:20.024.
  • DaviMSSon (Davi) of Williams secured P2 with a lap time of 1:20.144, merely 0.120 seconds behind the pole-sitter.
  • SarahMarinsS (Sarah) of Mercedes-AMG Petronas secured P3 with a lap time of 1:20.625, locking out the front-row sweep. The session was held under clear weather conditions, with ambient temperatures at 22°C and track temperatures stabilizing at 31°C. All competitors utilized the Soft compound dry-weather tyres (designated as Red 'S') for their fastest classification laps. 2. TECHNICAL NOTES AND SCRUTINEERING Following the conclusion of the qualifying session, all cars were weighed and subjected to selective inspections. In accordance with the official requests submitted prior to the GP of Australia, specialized scrutineering was conducted on Car No. 2 (Felipe, KICK Sauber). Specifically, the rear diffuser assembly, underfloor strakes, and engine mapping files were inspected to ensure full compliance with Articles 3.5.1 and 5.2 of the 2026 FIA Technical Regulations. Following thorough physical and digital analysis, the FIA Technical Delegate confirms that Car No. 2 complies fully with all regulatory frameworks. The aerodynamic and tyre management performance observed is deemed a result of compliant mechanical setup and vehicle development. Furthermore, track limit sensors at Turn 1, Turn 6, and Turn 11 were operational throughout the session. Several lap times were deleted during the session for track limit infringements, but all times listed in the official classification above are fully verified and compliant.
