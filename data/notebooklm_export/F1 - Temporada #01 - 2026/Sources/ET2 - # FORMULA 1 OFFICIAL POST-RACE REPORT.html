FORMULA 1 OFFICIAL POST-RACE REPORT

SAUDI ARABIAN GRAND PRIX: Tactical Masterclass as Felipe Stuns Jeddah with Sensational Win; Chaos Strikes Rising Stars

JEDDAH, Saudi Arabia — The unforgiving, high-speed concrete canyon of the Jeddah Corniche Circuit lived up to its chaotic reputation during the second round of the 2026 FIA Formula 1 World Championship. In a race defined by fierce wheel-to-wheel combat, barrier collisions, and strict FIA stewarding, Kick Sauber’s veteran rookie Felipe delivered an absolute masterclass of racecraft and survival to claim a stunning maiden victory, leaping to the top of the World Drivers' Championship standings.

🏆 RACE STORY: Survival of the Fittest in Jeddah

Under the dazzling night floodlights, the Saudi Arabian Grand Prix turned into an afternoon of high drama, particularly for the young Brazilian talents who had dazzled just weeks prior in Bahrain.

  • Felipe’s Tactical Masterclass (1st, 26 pts): Starting from a formidable grid position, Felipe avoided the chaotic skirmishes that plagued the narrow street circuit. Displaying the clinical precision of a seasoned engineer and race driver, he piloted his Kick Sauber machine flawlessly to cross the line first, securing 26 championship points and firing the Swiss-Italian outfit to the summit of the Constructors' Championship with 26 points.
  • Hamilton and Alonso Capitalize: Mercedes veteran Lewis Hamilton steered clear of trouble to claim a stellar P2 finish (18 pts), while Aston Martin’s Fernando Alonso rolled back the years with a relentless drive to secure the final step of the podium in P3 (15 pts).
  • The Drama of Jeddah (Sarah and Davi's Nightmare):
  • Sarah faced a turbulent evening on the streets of Jeddah. Plagued by early misfortune, a Lap 4 collision with Valtteri Bottas and a subsequent Lap 12 tangle with Nico Hülkenberg brought strict attention from the stewards, ultimately tumbling the Mercedes youngster down to a disappointing P20 finish.
  • Davi showed blistering pace in the Williams but fell victim to the razor-thin margins of the street circuit. Multiple track-limit infractions (resulting in 3-second time penalties on laps 3, 6, and 9) alongside a Lap 12 collision with Lewis Hamilton derailed his race, leaving him classified in P14.
