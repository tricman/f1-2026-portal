#### **FÉDÉRATION INTERNATIONALE DE L'AUTOMOBILE**

OFFICIAL TECHNICAL REPORT: ROUND 3 - GRAND PRIX OF AUSTRALIA (MELBOURNE) OFFICIAL RACE CLASSIFICATION & EVENTS LOG
**1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & TRACK REPORT Melbourne, Albert Park Circuit — The third round of the 2026 Formula 1 World Championship delivered a monumental and historically unprecedented outcome. Sarah (SarahMarinsS), driving for Mercedes-AMG Petronas,** captured an extraordinary victory from P3 on the grid, completing her rehabilitation after a difficult weekend in Jeddah. Sarah managed her tire strategy masterfully, completing a single-stop Medium-to-Hard run to finish first  **overall with a total race time of 22:20.897.**
**In second place, pole-sitter Felipe (frdsilva) secured 19 points for KICK Sauber (18 points for P2 plus 1 point for setting the overall Fastest Lap of the race with purple timing). Felipe's stellar performance further consolidates his position as a major title contender. A notable talking point occurred on Lap 11, when Sarah and Felipe suffered a** collision. Following a review by the race stewards, Sarah was handed an official Warning (Aviso) for the incident, though both vehicles sustained negligible damage and successfully maintained their positions at the front.
**The race was defined by early heartbreak for Davi (DaviMSSon) of Williams. Starting P2 on the front row, Davi received a track limits warning on Lap 3 for curve cutting before experiencing terminal damage on Lap 4, forcing an** early retirement (NC). Max Verstappen completed the podium in P3 for Red Bull Racing.
